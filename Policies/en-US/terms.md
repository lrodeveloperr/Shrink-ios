## What the app does

The app helps you record consumer products and compare package amounts and unit costs over time. You choose a supermarket for a shopping trip, scan a supported barcode or enter a product manually, and record the current package amount and shelf price.

The first saved check creates a baseline. A later comparable check can indicate less product, a higher unit cost, better value, no meaningful change, or that more information is needed. A price increase alone is not labeled shrinkflation when the package amount and item count did not decrease.

## How comparisons work

The app normalizes compatible quantities and can link changed barcodes within one saved product family. A package-size change can be detected without a price. Money impact is estimated only when the saved records have compatible supermarket, currency, channel, price type, and any recorded branch.

Results are informational estimates. They are not financial, legal, nutritional, or other professional advice and do not prove that a manufacturer or supermarket acted intentionally.
