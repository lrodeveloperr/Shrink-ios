## Description

Know what your dollar buys before you decide.

Shrinkflation Price Scanner helps you compare package amount and unit cost over time. Scan a barcode or enter a product manually, record today's package amount and shelf price, then save what you decided.

Your first check creates a baseline. On a later trip, the app compares the same product family and can show less product, a higher unit cost, better value, or no meaningful change.

CHECK THE EVIDENCE

- Look up supported barcodes in a built-in catalogue of 426,044 selected US branded-food products from USDA FoodData Central.
- Record in-store or online prices as regular, sale, or member prices.
- Link an unknown or changed barcode to a product you have already saved.
- Search and filter your on-device history.
- Edit decisions, delete checks, and undo a deletion for eight seconds.
- See estimated extra cost paid or avoided based on your recorded decision and quantity.

BUILT FOR THE US AND PUERTO RICO

Money is shown in dollars. The app supports English and Spanish tailored for Puerto Rico, with supermarket choices ordered for the detected US or Puerto Rico region.

PRIVACY AND ACCESS

No account is required. The catalogue and comparison tools use data bundled with the app. Saved price checks are stored locally on the device. Camera permission is used only when you choose to scan a barcode or shelf-price label.

The app includes a fixed banner. An optional one-time in-app purchase removes that banner. The localized purchase price appears in the app.
